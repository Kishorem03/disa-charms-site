1. `index.html` — **Home Page** (Hero Showcase, 12 Signature Collections Grid, Featured Restocked Products, Floating WhatsApp Chat).
2. `catalog.html` — **Product Showcase Catalog** (Interactive filtering across all 12 Collections, Multi-Image Thumbnail Switcher per card, Size selector, and Dynamic WhatsApp Inquiry with Product ID & Image Ref).
3. `about.html` — **316L Anti-Tarnish Craft & Heritage** (Waterproof, Sweatproof, and Hypoallergenic promise).
4. `contact.html` — **Visit Erode Store & Location Map** (Embedded Google Map for Perundurai Rd, Near Zudio, Opp. Westside, Erode + Direct WhatsApp link).
5. `admin.html` — **Client Login & Multi-Image Upload Portal** (Demo credentials: `disa.charms` / `disa2025` — upload up to 5 images per product, assign categories & sizes, and configure the store WhatsApp number).
6. `admin/config.yml` — **Decap CMS Configuration** for Git-based static hosting on Netlify/Cloudflare Pages.
